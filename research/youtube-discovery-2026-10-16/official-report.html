Agreed rule

An official category or whole-word “official” in a video title supplies the authority evidence for automatic canonical admission after artist/song identity and song-music-video content checks pass. Uncertain identity/content remains reviewable; official audio, live performances, lyrics, visualizers and other excluded content remain excluded. “Unofficial” does not match.

Research only: no application implementation, canonical records, approvals, downloads or motion checks were made. Every surviving item below still needs identity/song and visual verification.

What this adds

  • Three official playlists contain 39 entries: Pop Will Eat Itself 19, Mad Caddies four, The Fall 16.
  • 25 entries gain official authority from the category alone: 12 Pop Will Eat Itself videos and 13 The Fall videos have no “official” in their own titles.
  • Mad Caddies’ Official Music Videos playlist includes New Song (Sublime Cover) Official Lyric Video. It fails retained content checks despite the playlist category.
  • John Coltrane has 219 artist-related keyword hits, all rejected by the existing title/uploader content checks, chiefly Official Audio. Keyword discovery must feed those checks.
  • Mixed playlists yield many official titles by unrelated artists. They require identity resolution, not automatic assignment to the page’s artist. Dave Brubeck solo-versus-Quartet credits also remain unresolved by keyword alone.
  • The literal English word does not include Luis Miguel’s Spanish Video Oficial results. Those remain outside this specific rule unless localized equivalents are added.
