Vidarr YouTube discovery audit

15 artists · 16 October 2026 · Research for review before implementation

11/15 artists: no standard Videos tab93 expanded playlists across artist audits53 watch metadata samples9 artists with strong metadata candidates

Finding

Vidarr currently uses one standard YouTube uploads listing. It converts the saved Music channel URL into www.youtube.com/channel/…/videos. For Pop Will Eat Itself this produces “This channel does not have a videos tab,” which is converted into a successful empty result. The Music page and its curated official-video playlist are never traversed.

Pop Will Eat Itself’s Official Music Videos playlist contains 19 returned entries. Including the separate Music video shelf yields 20 distinct linked IDs. Their actual publishers include the artist and VEVO. A Topic discovery-page label should not reject every linked video: the actual uploader matters.

Scope: Read-only public-page/flat-metadata audit; no application implementation, catalog changes, approvals, downloads, motion samples or imports. These are metadata findings. No video has been visually certified as an official song music video.

Method and coverage

96 confirmed artists had zero catalog videos and linked channels; 78 already had assigned YouTube review rows. Sample focuses on the remaining 18 with zero assigned YouTube candidates: target 5574 plus 14 selected by deterministic SHA256 seed vidarr-youtube-audit-2026-10-16:<id>. Not representative of all artists.

Enumerated saved-channel Videos/Playlists tabs, Shorts/Live when advertised, Music video and artist-created-playlist shelves, linked playlist contents, and Music View all continuations. Matched uploader channels received complete flat upload listings and selected music-video playlist expansion. Searches were bounded to eight results per query; supplemental identity/language searches were used for D.I., Hüsker Dü and Luis Miguel. All counts describe returned metadata, not approved music videos. Generated playlists may be finite YouTube snapshots; unavailable/private/deleted entries and failed endpoints limit coverage.

All Music “View all” creator-playlist continuations found in this sample completed: The Fall added eight playlists; John Coltrane added 29. Every expanded playlist’s return count and exit code is included below; failed/unavailable endpoints are retained in machine-readable summary. The standard/Music home pages were examined independently. This is complete traversal of the selected public surfaces returned during the audit, not an exhaustive search of all YouTube or an assessment of every listed video.

Decisions to review before building

  1. Broaden discovery: retain Music URLs; traverse Videos shelves, artist-created playlists and View all continuations. Follow relevant actual uploader channels, including labels. Deduplicate by video ID after collecting provenance.
  2. Constrain identity: preserve canonical artist/song checks; use credited artist and song context for D.I. and other ambiguous names. Playlist membership and a matching channel name alone cannot establish identity.
  3. Rank evidence, preserve content checks: Music OMV, artist/label/VEVO provenance, localized “Video Oficial” wording and promo descriptions are useful signals. OMV also identified live performances and visualizers in this sample. Title words and moving pictures alone do not prove an official song video.
  4. Explain empty results: distinguish missing Videos tab, removed channel, exhausted discovery, waiting review, and reviews assigned under another artist. Dave Brubeck Quartet currently shares a source with Dave Brubeck; 64 initial linked review rows already sit under Dave Brubeck. The current global external-ID lookup can reassign pending rows during sync; this is a code risk, not proof of the historical cause.
  5. Keep established stages: IMVDb remains the primary canonical catalog. YouTube discovery supplies candidates; exclusions, identity review, uncertain-item human review, visual checks, full-file import validation and delivery confirmation remain required.

Suggested first scope for discussion: Music shelves + curated playlists + clear coverage status. Label/source expansion, multilingual ranking and shared-artist attribution can then be reviewed with these examples.
